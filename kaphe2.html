Who we are

Kape. Cà phê. Kaphê.

The name Kaphê blends two words for coffee: the Filipino “kape” and the Vietnamese “cà phê.” It reflects both of our heritages — a blend of where we come from and what we're creating together.

We wanted to pay homage to our roots not just in name, but in menu too. Every pour is inspired by flavors, traditions, and stories from both cultures. Kaphê isn't just about drinks. It's about connection, culture, and sharing something meaningful with our community.

Two friends. One dream. Zero doubt.

For years, we were asked: if you didn't work a 9-to-5, what would you do? The answer was always, “I want to own a little peaceful café and retire.” Why wait for permission to be ready when being ready to chase a dream is a choice we can make now?

So here we are — figuring it out as we go, sharing the wins, mistakes, tasting experiments, and laughter along the way. Come join us as we fulfill a dream that once only existed in conversation.
